How It Works

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What file formats are supported?
    PDF Side Note works exclusively with PDF files. If your slides are in PowerPoint (.pptx) or Keynote format, export them as a PDF first using your presentation software, then upload the resulting PDF here.
  • Is there a file size limit?
    You can upload PDF files up to 100 MB in size. This is more than enough for most lecture slide decks and presentations, even those with high-resolution images.
  • What is the difference between grid and ruled lines?
    Square grids provide a matrix of horizontal and vertical lines, ideal for diagrams, graphs, and structured notes. Ruled lines are horizontal only, similar to a lined notebook, and work best for written text. The spacing option lets you choose how far apart the lines are — smaller values mean finer lines.
  • Are my uploaded files stored on the server?
    No. Your PDF is processed entirely in memory. It is never saved to disk or stored on our servers. Once the converted file is sent back to your browser, all data from your upload is discarded.
  • What paper size is the output?
    The output is A4 landscape (297 x 210 mm). Each page places your original slide on the left half and the note area on the right half. This layout is optimized for standard A4 printers and works well for binder filing.
  • Can I use this on my phone or tablet?
    Yes. PDF Side Note is fully responsive and works in any modern browser on any device. Simply open the website, upload your file, and the converted PDF will be downloaded to your device.
  • Do I need to create an account?
    No. There is no registration or login required. The tool is completely free and open to everyone with no usage limits or restrictions.
