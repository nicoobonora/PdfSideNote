Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Side Note ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and PDF conversion service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our service:

  • Uploaded files: When you use the conversion tool, your PDF file is sent to our server for processing. The file is held in memory only for the duration of the conversion and is never written to permanent storage.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics such as the number of conversions performed. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.
  • Cookies: Our website itself does not set cookies. However, third-party services such as Google AdSense may use cookies to serve relevant advertisements. See the "Third-Party Services" section below for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your uploaded PDF file is used solely to generate the converted output file with note space. We do not read, analyze, or extract any content from your files beyond what is necessary for the conversion process. We do not share your files with any third parties.

3. File Retention

We do not store your uploaded or converted files. All file data is processed in server memory (RAM) and is discarded immediately after the converted PDF is sent back to your browser. No copies are retained on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information transmitted to and from our service. File uploads are processed over HTTPS to ensure data is encrypted in transit. Since we do not store files on disk, there is no risk of stored file data being compromised.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF Side Note is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data or uploaded files, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have questions about your data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact information on our About page.
